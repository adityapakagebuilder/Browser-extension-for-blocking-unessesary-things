Clear your Gmail promos

Connect Gmail to remove the promo emails already in your mailbox. Verification and sign-in codes, starred mail and senders you choose are kept. Removed mail goes to Trash for 30 days.

Gmail access isn't set up in this build yet. See “Gmail setup” in the README.

Open the side panel

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Promo Shield.
  2. Click the Promo Shield icon. The panel opens on the right side of the browser.
  3. Turn the ad blocker and other filters on or off there at any time.
